  --- 0.5.4.425 — Molotov fire overhaul (DEPLOYED 2026-07-09, commit f2f606b) ---
  All in step_fire_patches (src/game/state.rs) + the fire render section
  (src/renderer/loop_runner.rs) + new src/renderer/wa_sprites.rs. Covered by
  tests/fire_reaction.rs (3 tests) and tests/flame_render.rs; parity 23/23.
  1. Flame visuals: the procedural teardrop flame is replaced by a 32-frame
     animated flame sprite (flicker loop, then a shrink-to-spark burn-out as the
     patch expires), with two variants so neighbouring flames aren't in lockstep.
     Baked assets src/renderer/wa_sprites/flame1.bin, flame2.bin; loader
     wa_sprites.rs. Render-only (reads the already-synced FirePatch fields), so
     all five paths match with no StateMsg/net changes.
  2. Molotov no longer craters. apply_explosion_scaled now skips terrain carving,
     the crater_log push, and the dirt-fallout FX for WeaponKind::MolotovCocktail
     — a dug-out pit plus the fire pool was an inescapable death trap. The fire
     does the work; the blast keeps its light direct damage/knockback. (Skipping
     crater_log keeps clients crater-free too, so no desync.)
  3. Burn damage is capped PER SOLDIER (1 HP / 8 ticks ≈ 3.75 HP/s) instead of per
     fire patch — a molotov spawns ~48 overlapping patches, so the old per-patch
     damage stacked into an instant kill.
  4. Burning soldiers now escape the fire instead of getting stuck and dying:
     - they HOP periodically (every 14 ticks; HOP_VX 4.0, HOP_VY 4.0) and slide
       between hops, all in ONE committed direction latched at ignition (away from
       the blast centre, stored in the existing Soldier.facing field — no new
       synced state).
     - if a barrier (tall terrain / barrel / mine) blocks the committed way, the
       hop flips to the opposite direction and commits to it, so a soldier wedged
       against a wall keeps trying the other way out.
     - burning soldiers pass THROUGH other soldiers mid-hop (they still land on
       terrain), so a clump jammed together on a small peak can hop apart.
     Note: an earlier attempt used a downhill-slide term, removed here — fire
     pools in low spots, so "slide downhill" dragged soldiers back into the flames
     and they oscillated in place.

     standable spot as a last resort before giving up.

  --- New weapon: Robot (WA Sheep-style autonomous walker) ---
  WeaponKind::Robot. Placed like TNT (instant, no aim/charge). Walks/climbs 0-8px
  steps; when blocked by something taller it launches a single automatic
  obstacle-clearing jump (ballistic arc, terrain-detection driven, not player
  input) and reverses only if it lands still blocked — matches WA's Sheep. 10s
  fixed real-time fuse, ticking unconditionally every tick regardless of whose
  turn/phase it is; numeric countdown appears over its head for the final 5s.
  Detonates on fuse expiry, water contact, being caught in another explosion, or
  a manual A-press by the placing team during their own turn (server only
  forwards the currently-active team's input into simulation, so true any-time
  off-turn remote detonation isn't wired — same-turn convenience only). The
  placing team's own turn holds in Watching (not Retreating) until it
  detonates, scoped to owner_team so it never blocks an unrelated team's turn.
  Rare-tier crate drop. 75 max damage / 45 blast radius / 18 knockback. In-world
  sprite has a 2-frame walk-cycle leg animation, glowing eyes, mouth, antenna;
  matching robot-head weapon-menu icon (also given a mouth). Fully synced
  (RobotState/NetRobot/parity checklist in net_sync.rs + tests/parity.rs,
  robot_state_parity test covers walking + falling sub-states).
  Two real bugs found post-implementation and fixed:
    1. Jump never actually happened. The fix that added an immediate ballistic
       step when the jump starts called robot_snap_to_surface, which re-landed
       the robot right back on the ground it just launched from (only ~5px up,
       still within the 10px landing-scan window) — canceling the jump before
       it ever visibly moved, and testing horizontal clearance at the pre-jump
       ground height (always still "blocked" by the same obstacle) rather than
       the risen height.
    2. Fixed by only checking for a landing surface once past the arc's apex
       (vel_y > 0 — by then it's ~20px clear of the launch point, well outside
       the 10px snap window) and testing clearance at the post-move y; also
       stopped permanently zeroing vel_x on a blocked tick (it now just pauses
       horizontal motion for that tick and resumes once risen high enough),
       since the old code killed horizontal motion for the rest of the jump
       the instant it was blocked even once early in the arc.

  --- Explosion direct-hit bonus REMOVED for all weapons ---
  apply_explosion_scaled (state.rs) used to add a flat +20 (capped at 99)
  whenever a soldier was within 10px of the blast center, for every explosive
  except Blasthive/Bazooka/HomingMissile. Removed entirely, no exceptions —
  max_damage() is now the true ceiling for every weapon regardless of distance.

  --- Movement smoothing over curved terrain (crater lips, hills) ---
  snap_to_surface and is_on_ground (loop_runner.rs) widened from 3 fixed probe
  columns (left/center/right edge) to the full soldier body width, so they
  agree with try_move_horizontal's placement check (already full-width) — a
  curve's true peak falling between the 3 old sample points no longer gets
  missed, which used to read as a jerky extra step when crossing a crater lip
  or hill. try_move_horizontal itself needed no change.

  --- WA mask library grown 12->18 (14 island, 4 cavern) ---
  New masks extracted from fresh MapGen.exe output under Wine/Xvfb — its
  headless CLI (-t TYPE -o FILE -w) turned out much simpler than driving the
  full game to generate land.dat. MapGen's preview PNG bakes in decorative
  sprites (trees/cacti/skulls) though, so built a cleanup pass (morphological
  opening via PIL MinFilter/MaxFilter to strip thin decoration stalks, then a
  connected-component pass keeping only border-touching/large components) to
  recover the real terrain silhouette. New masks chosen for genuinely curvy/
  rounded contours (rolling hills, wavy canyons, blobby cave chambers) vs. the
  more angular examples already in the library.

  --- Spawn placement fixes ---
  (a) Cavern clustering bug: the cavern spawn pass used a strict single-pass
  separation scan that silently skipped a soldier outright once a chamber was
  too small to fit everyone with full MIN_SEP separation — and the fallback
  logic (built for open-air/surface terrain, standable_foot_levels requires
  ~100px of open sky) couldn't rescue cavern maps, so skipped soldiers
  defaulted to the same fixed point. Replaced with the same greedy
  maximize-worst-case-separation dispersion pattern already used in the
  "last resort" pass elsewhere in find_team_spawns, so a cramped chamber now
  spreads its team as far apart as it actually allows, never duplicates.
  (b) MIN_SEP/MIN_SEP_V loosened 140/120 -> 70/60px. Dynamic analysis of real
  WA (launched WA_patched.exe live under Wine/Xvfb, started an actual
  Deathmatch) showed WA does NOT enforce meaningful separation between a
  team's worms — observed "Prince Charles"/"Prince Andrew" (same team) spawn
  ~20-30px apart on the same ledge. Kept a modest floor rather than matching
  WA's fully loose placement (a centred blast still can't reliably gut two
  teammates at once), but allow tighter WA-like clustering than before.
  (c) Scenery clearance margin widened footprint+10 -> footprint+24px so two
  nearby decorations' exclusion zones merge and swallow gaps too narrow to
  actually stand/move in, instead of leaving a technically-clear sliver
  between them that individually satisfied each object's own check.
  (d) Barrels now also check horizontal scenery clearance (too_close_to_scenery,
  main.rs) — they always checked landing Y against scenery (surface_y_at_
  with_scenery, so they rest on top instead of embedding) but never checked X
  clearance, so a barrel could land stacked directly against/on top of a
  decoration with no visual gap.

  --- Stuck-soldier turn-lock fix ---
  Blast knockback into a cramped cavern ceiling could embed a soldier in solid
  terrain; the airborne swept-movement loop reverted to that exact (embedded)
  spot every tick since its very first sub-step was already blocked — the
  soldier never landed, state stayed Airborne forever, and the all_grounded
  turn-advance gate in simulate_with_muzzle could never fire. Added
  unstick_embedded_soldier watchdog at the top of the Airborne physics step:
  nudges the soldier up or down (whichever clears first, up to SOLDIER_H+24px)
  before running normal physics; forces Idle if truly nowhere clear is found
  nearby rather than hanging the turn forever.

  --- Boulder (Rugged scenery) height reduced 48px -> 33px ---
  base_footprint (12,16) -> (12,11) in terrain.rs. draw_boulder's gray rock was
  just over the ~46px backflip apex height, making it un-climbable. Confirmed
  via a new labeled scenery reference gallery (see below) after having to guess
  which sprite a "gray dome" bug report meant from code/colors alone.

  --- Weapon menu scroll fix ---
  Up/Down navigation (process_weapon_menu, loop_runner.rs) used to wrap as
  soon as it hit the CURRENT column's own item count — columns can be shorter
  than the grid when the weapon count isn't a multiple of 4 (column 0 is
  always full-length by construction; later columns can be shorter) — so a
  short column could never reach a row that only exists in a longer column,
  and since the on-screen scroll follows the cursor's row, the view could get
  stuck short of the bottom even though longer columns had unseen rows below.
  Now falls back to column 0 (guaranteed to have every row) whenever the
  current column doesn't have an item at the target row.

  --- New dev tool: scenery gallery ---
  cargo run --bin scenery-gallery (src/bin/scenery_gallery.rs, new Cargo.toml

  ROBOT_JUMP_VX 2.2->3.8 so the obstacle-clearing hop covers ~40-60px

  --- 0.5.4.421 — bazooka wind-hook, crate-camera fix, damage-tally turn-end
      fix (DEPLOYED 2026-07-07, commit 04240d8; renumbered from a same-day
      build originally tagged .422) ---
  BAZOOKA WIND-HOOK. Added `BAZOOKA_WIND_SCALE = 0.32` (src/physics/tick.rs) —
  4x the shared `WIND_SCALE = 0.08` — applied only to `WeaponKind::Bazooka`;
  every other wind-affected weapon (SuperSheep) keeps the original constant.
  This is deliberately NOT a revival of the reverted .421 physics port below:
  gravity, launch speed, and charge rate are untouched, only the wind
  acceleration term changed, and the linear/velocity-independent model itself
  is kept exactly as-is (matches real WA wind behavior — constant per-tick
  acceleration, not drag, ignores projectile speed). At strong wind this lets
  a bazooka visibly curve/hook around terrain corners, and in extreme cases
  (slow or near-vertical shots) reverse horizontal direction mid-flight and
  drift back toward the shooter — all emergent from the same simple model
  WA itself uses, just tuned strong enough to be felt. Value is a playtest
  starting point, not derived from a measurement — tune up/down as needed.
  CRATE-CAMERA FIX. The crate-watch phase (camera follows a falling supply
  crate) exited on a fixed 90-tick timer regardless of whether the crate had
  actually landed, so the camera could cut to the active soldier while a
  crate was still mid-air (e.g. a re-triggered fall when terrain under a
  landed crate gets carved out). Now also holds while
  `game.crates.iter().any(|c| !c.landed)` (loop_runner.rs).
  DAMAGE-TALLY TURN-END FIX. `TurnPhase::Ending` only blocked turn advance on
  the active soldier being airborne; it didn't check whether damage numbers
  were still tallying/counting down for *any* soldier. Now also holds while
  `pending_damage > 0 || damage_settle > 0 || hp_countdown_delay > 0` on any
  soldier (loop_runner.rs), so turns can no longer end mid-popup or mid-HP-
  drain.
  All three changes live entirely inside `simulate_with_muzzle`/`tick()`,
  which per the "Automatic parity for simulation features" rule in CLAUDE.md
  already runs identically across all 5 game paths — confirmed by
  `cargo test --test parity` (22/22 passing), no new StateMsg fields needed.

KEY ARCHITECTURE NOTES
-----------------------
  simulate_with_muzzle(game, input, muzzle, aim_angle) — single sim function
    called by all 5 paths. aim_angle: Option<f32> lets server pass received angle
    directly; Up/Down buttons are never stripped and flow to all weapons.
  StateMsg round-trip — live client rebuilds state from server's StateMsg each tick.
    Never runs simulate. Enforced by compile-time checklists in net_sync.rs.
  emit_fx / emit_sound — all particle FX and sounds go through these channels
    to auto-replicate to live clients. Never call fx functions directly from sim.
  assert_all_paths_in_sync — parity test helper in tests/parity.rs. Call for
    every new gameplay feature test.
  Framebuffer blit (Miyoo): rows reversed into heap flip_buf, flushed to mmap
    in one copy per frame. Screen is 640x480, rotated 180° in hardware.
