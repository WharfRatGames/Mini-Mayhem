0.5.4.429  Map generation refined against a bigger reference set: maps now carry noticeably more land mass and taller, more dramatic relief (the old generator was quietly capping cliff height). Nearly every column of the map has ground again — no more huge empty ocean stretches mid-map; wide canyons now end in a floored pit instead of cutting straight to the sea (narrow jumpable gaps can still drown you). Sparse "confetti" seeds are gone: small debris chunks are cleaned up, but any land that's the only footing in its area is always kept. More and bigger overhang ledges. Plus 9 new scenery decorations: birdhouse, watering can, pumpkin, anvil, totem pole, firewood stack, treasure chest, ore boulder with pickaxe, and a cluster of lit candles.
0.5.4.428  Map generation overhaul: every map silhouette library entry has been rebuilt from cleaner source art, so terrain no longer sprouts floating debris chunks, scaffold shapes or stray props. Open-air maps now always end in water on both sides, with thinner edge gaps and more of the map playable. Terrain is more varied: taller relief, rougher natural surfaces, chunkier overhang arches, and far fewer floating islands. Barrel fire now burns about a third as deep into the ground. The gray boulder's looks now match its hitbox, so soldiers stand on top of it instead of wading through it (barrels and mines rest on top too). Bone pile and ribcage decorations shrunk to sensible sizes; pine tree and cairns no longer have see-through gaps; mushroom caps and sunflowers show their true colours. 15 new scenery decorations across all map themes: hay bale, scarecrow, stone well, wheelbarrow, beehive, menhir, signpost, campfire, cartwheel, ram skull, glowing mushrooms, stalagmite, minecart, geode and lantern post.
