0.5.4.434  New weapon — the Jackhammer: a pneumatic drill that bores straight down through the ground, sinking your soldier into a fresh vertical shaft and damaging anyone caught in it. Press A to start drilling and A again to stop early. Starts with 3 per loadout and also turns up in common weapon crates.
0.5.4.433  Map relief tuning: open-air terrain now carries a touch more vertical variation between hilltops and valleys, so silhouettes feel a little more dramatic and varied without pushing cliffs past what soldiers can still climb.
0.5.4.432  More map variety: a new family of denser "base-and-guns" style terrain — bigger, chunkier land masses with rolling hilltops — now shows up on about a quarter of open-air maps. Plus 8 new open-air layouts and 8 new cavern layouts added to the existing rotation, so maps feel less repetitive from match to match.
0.5.4.431  The Robot weapon is now the Jumpbot: it hops forward in little leaps instead of gliding, vaulting obstacles and reversing at walls, at a retuned walking pace. The plasma torch now pauses the turn timer while it's running, so you no longer lose your turn mid-tunnel. Barrel fires now set off any armed mines they touch. Ninja rope overhauled: swing momentum carries through when you let go and can be rebuilt by re-attaching in mid-air, corner-wraps slingshot you around, the rope reaches farther and is drawn thicker with a pointed tip, and you get 5 ropes per loadout. Also fixed a rope bug where a soldier could get stuck being dragged with the turn never ending.
0.5.4.430  Scenery (trees, rocks, and other props) is now real solid terrain, not just decoration — walking, explosions, bullets, and the plasma torch all interact with it exactly like dirt (fixes the torch stalling in front of scenery instead of always carving through). Map generation cleaned up further: caverns almost always form as one connected chamber like they should, and open-air maps hold together better too. Barrel fire now eats much less ground per hit and less often overall.
0.5.4.429  Map generation refined against a bigger reference set: maps now carry noticeably more land mass and taller, more dramatic relief (the old generator was quietly capping cliff height). Nearly every column of the map has ground again — no more huge empty ocean stretches mid-map; wide canyons now end in a floored pit instead of cutting straight to the sea (narrow jumpable gaps can still drown you). Sparse "confetti" seeds are gone: small debris chunks are cleaned up, but any land that's the only footing in its area is always kept. More and bigger overhang ledges. Plus 9 new scenery decorations: birdhouse, watering can, pumpkin, anvil, totem pole, firewood stack, treasure chest, ore boulder with pickaxe, and a cluster of lit candles.
0.5.4.428  Map generation overhaul: every map silhouette library entry has been rebuilt from cleaner source art, so terrain no longer sprouts floating debris chunks, scaffold shapes or stray props. Open-air maps now always end in water on both sides, with thinner edge gaps and more of the map playable. Terrain is more varied: taller relief, rougher natural surfaces, chunkier overhang arches, and far fewer floating islands. Barrel fire now burns about a third as deep into the ground. The gray boulder's looks now match its hitbox, so soldiers stand on top of it instead of wading through it (barrels and mines rest on top too). Bone pile and ribcage decorations shrunk to sensible sizes; pine tree and cairns no longer have see-through gaps; mushroom caps and sunflowers show their true colours. 15 new scenery decorations across all map themes: hay bale, scarecrow, stone well, wheelbarrow, beehive, menhir, signpost, campfire, cartwheel, ram skull, glowing mushrooms, stalagmite, minecart, geode and lantern post.
